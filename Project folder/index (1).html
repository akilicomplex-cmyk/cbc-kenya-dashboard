The 2‑6‑3‑3 pathway

Pre‑primary through senior secondary
01

Pre‑Primary

2 years

PP1–PP2. Play‑based foundations in language, numeracy, and motor skills before Grade 1.

02

Primary

6 years

Grades 1–6, split into lower and upper primary, building core literacy, numeracy and life‑skill competencies.

03

Junior Secondary

3 years

Grades 7–9. Learners begin choosing subject clusters ahead of the senior‑school pathway decision.

04

Senior Secondary

3 years

Grades 10–12 across STEM, Social Sciences, and Arts & Sports Science pathways, leading to tertiary or work.
